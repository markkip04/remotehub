About RemoteHub

My name is Mark Kipyegon Rotich. I am an undergraduate student at the University of Nairobi studying BSc. Microprocessor Technology and Instrumentation.

Like many university students, I faced the harsh reality of campus life and financial hardship. Desperate to find a way to sustain myself and cover my expenses, I fell victim to fake job scams that promised easy remote money but delivered nothing.

However, I didn't give up. I pivoted, started working with Opera Bloggers to earn a living, and over the last 2 years, I have dedicated my time to deep researching the remote work industry. I have tested, applied to, and worked on most of the legitimate platforms listed on this website.

Why I built this site: I created RemoteHub to protect others from the scams I fell for. This site is a collection of my real, tested research. I do not offer fake jobs, I do not ask for money, and I do not sell data. I simply show you the real platforms that pay, how much they actually pay, and exactly how to pass their strict application processes.

If you are a student, a job seeker, or anyone looking for honest remote work, you are in the right place.
