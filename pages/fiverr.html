Fiverr Guide: How to Actually Make Money

Published: November 2026 | Category: Freelancing

Fiverr is the easiest place to start freelancing, but the competition is fierce. Here is how to stand out, rank your gig, and get your first client.

How Fiverr Works

Unlike traditional job boards, on Fiverr, you create "Gigs" (services you offer) and buyers come to you. You can sell anything from logo design to voiceovers, coding, or virtual assistance.

⚠️ The 20% Fee Rule

Fiverr takes a 20% cut of every job you complete. If you charge $100, you keep $80. Keep this in mind when pricing your services. Also, never take communication off the platform early, or you will get banned.

How to Rank on Page 1

1. Use a video: Gigs with videos get 40% more clicks.
2. Niche down: Don't just say "I do graphic design." Say "I design minimalist real estate logos."
3. Fast response time: The algorithm rewards sellers who reply within 1 hour.
