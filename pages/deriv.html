Deriv Trading: The Harsh Reality

Category: Trading | High Risk

⚠️ STOP AND READ THIS FIRST

1. Payment is NOT guaranteed. You can and will lose your money if you do not know what you are doing.

2. This is NOT a quick way to make money. It requires deep economic knowledge (interest rates, CPI, news) and understanding market trends.

3. Gambling Addiction: Binary options and high-leverage Forex are extremely addictive. If you find yourself chasing losses, STOP immediately. Trade only what you can afford to lose.

Deriv is a legitimate, regulated broker. They do pay out. But that does not mean it is easy. 70% to 90% of retail traders lose money.

Why Deriv Pays

Deriv is not a scam. They are a real broker offering contracts for difference (CFDs) and synthetic indices. They make their money from spreads and commissions. If you win, they pay you (minus their fees).

Why you will likely lose money (The 90% Rule)

You cannot just look at the green and red candles. The market moves based on news, economic data, and liquidity. If you jump into Deriv expecting to turn $10 into $1000 in a day, you are gambling, not trading.

How to Actually Learn

1. Use the Demo Account: Deriv gives you a free virtual account. Do not deposit a single shilling until you can consistently double your demo account over 3 months.

2. Learn Economics: Read about Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP), CPI, and interest rates.

3. Risk Management: Never risk more than 1% of your total account on a single trade.
