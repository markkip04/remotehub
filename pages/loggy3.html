Published: November 2026 | Category: AI Training | Available in Kenya

Loggy3 pays you to let an app record your computer screen. It is real and it pays in Kenya, but it is NOT easy money. Here is the honest breakdown.

⚠️ CRITICAL SECURITY WARNING

This app records everything on your screen, including what you type.

If you type a password, log into your bank, or check private messages while recording is ON, that information is recorded. You MUST stop the recording before typing any passwords. Never use your main banking laptop for this. Use a separate computer if possible.

How Much Does Loggy3 Actually Pay?

You can get promoted to Apprentice ($0.25/hr), Advanced ($0.50/hr), or Pro ($1.00/hr) based on the quality of work you record (e.g., coding and Excel are better than watching YouTube) [citation:1][citation:4].

Payouts are sent on the 1st and 15th of every month via PayPal or Dots. There is no minimum withdrawal amount [citation:1].
