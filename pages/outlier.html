Published: November 2026 | Category: AI Training

Outlier is one of the highest-paying AI training platforms right now. But getting accepted and staying on the platform requires strategy. Here is the honest breakdown of how it works and how much you can actually make.

What is Outlier?

Outlier connects subject matter experts with AI companies. You will typically be asked to read AI-generated text, write your own responses, and rate the AI on how accurate or helpful it is. It is completely remote and you set your own hours.
